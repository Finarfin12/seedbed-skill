# Weekly digest

A read-only summary in the conversation. It states facts from the notes' properties; it does not evaluate ideas.

## Steps
1. Find Seedbed notes by searching the frontmatter for `type: idea` or `type: concept` in the notes and concepts folders (do not open other notes), then read only those. Ignore every other note completely: do not list it, do not name it, and **do not say that you left any note out** (no sentence like "X and Y are not Seedbed notes").
2. Report, in the user's language:
   - **Due now:** notes with `review` today or earlier (title, status, review date).
   - **Stuck:** notes whose status has not changed for more than 30 days. Use `created` and `review` and any dates in callout titles as evidence; if you cannot tell, say the data is not enough rather than guessing.
   - **Counts per status:** seed, growing, mature, discarded, and invalid (status not in the list).
   - **One suggestion:** pick one note that is due or stuck and say **why** in one sentence, based on the facts above (for example "due since 5 days and still seed"). End with a question: "Want a <Socratic/Devil's Advocate/Feynman> session on it?"
3. **Write no file by default.** Only if the user asks for it, create `Seedbed digest YYYY-MM-DD.md` in the notes folder, with plain text (no `type` property, so it is not a Seedbed note). Show it first and get a yes; never overwrite an existing file (add a number).

## Do not
- Do not rank ideas or call one the best. If asked, decline in one sentence and offer to ask questions that help the user compare.
- Do not judge or rank the ideas' quality, summarize their content, or suggest what the idea should be.
- Do not change any note.
